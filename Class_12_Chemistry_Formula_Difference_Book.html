1️⃣ Solutions

BASIC

Concentration Terms

Molarity:
M = moles of solute / volume of solution (L)
Molality:
m = moles of solute / mass of solvent (kg)
Mole Fraction:
X₁ = n₁ / (n₁ + n₂)
Mass %:
Mass % = (mass of solute / mass of solution) × 100
INTERMEDIATE

Raoult's Law

p₁ = X₁p₁°
P = X₁p₁° + X₂p₂°
(p° − p)/p° = X₂
ADVANCED

Colligative Properties

ΔTᵦ = Kᵦm
ΔT𝒇 = K𝒇m
π = CRT
i = observed colligative property / calculated colligative property
ΔTᵦ = iKᵦm
ΔT𝒇 = iK𝒇m
π = iCRT

3️⃣ Chemical Kinetics

BASIC

Rate of Reaction

Rate = −Δ[R]/Δt
For aA → bB: Rate = −(1/a)Δ[A]/Δt = (1/b)Δ[B]/Δt
INTERMEDIATE

Rate Law

Rate = k[A]ᵐ[B]ⁿ
Overall order = m + n
ADVANCED

Integrated Rate Equations

Zero order: [R] = [R]₀ − kt
Zero order: t₁/₂ = [R]₀/2k
First order: k = (2.303/t) log([R]₀/[R])
First order: t₁/₂ = 0.693/k
Arrhenius: k = Ae^(−Ea/RT)
log(k₂/k₁) = Ea/(2.303R) × (T₂−T₁)/(T₁T₂)

⚖️ Zero Order vs First Order

Zero OrderFirst Order
Rate = kRate = k[A]
t₁/₂ = [A]₀/2kt₁/₂ = 0.693/k
Half-life depends on initial concentrationHalf-life independent of initial concentration

4️⃣ d & f Block Elements

BASIC

Electronic Configuration

d-block: (n−1)d¹⁻¹⁰ ns⁰⁻²
Lanthanides: [Xe] 4f¹⁻¹⁴ 5d⁰⁻¹ 6s²

Colour: Transition-metal compounds can be coloured because of electronic transitions involving d-orbitals.
Variable oxidation states: ns and (n−1)d orbital energies are relatively close.

5️⃣ Coordination Compounds

BASIC

Basic Terms

Coordination number: Number of donor atoms directly attached to the central metal ion.
Ligand: Ion or molecule capable of donating an electron pair to a central metal atom/ion.
INTERMEDIATE

Types of Ligands

TypeExample
MonodentateNH₃, H₂O, Cl⁻
Bidentateen, C₂O₄²⁻
PolydentateEDTA⁴⁻
AmbidentateNO₂⁻, SCN⁻
ADVANCED

Important Concepts

EAN = Atomic number − Oxidation state + electrons donated by ligands
Octahedral: d-orbitals split into t₂g and eg sets.
Tetrahedral: d-orbitals split into e and t₂ sets.

⚖️ Double Salt vs Coordination Compound

Double SaltCoordination Compound
